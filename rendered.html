What changed

The incident’s “one issue, one fix” claim is contradicted by its own computed views. A shared subject is a symptom, not a cause. Different subjects can describe the same bug; identical subjects can describe different bugs. Intake tags use the browser locale, which can differ from the tenant’s date convention. They are guesses, not ground truth.

Problems and ownership

  • Parser — engineering ticket: the shared parser interprets dates month first, even for day-first tenants. Day and month swap on the shared parsing path. Fix parsing against the tenant’s convention and verify the booking-to-invite round trip. Example: Harbour Dental, R-41200, 7 April becomes 4 July.
  • Form hint — engineering ticket: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY, but the form reads a day-first tenant’s input day first. Users follow the hint and get the wrong date. Fix the hint and form contract together. Affected form tenants: Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour. Example: R-41230, Copper Studio, form channel. This needs its own fix and ticket.

The queue did not suddenly start in July

The received and merged timestamps measure different events. Reports arrived from April onward; a large July merge is backlog handling, not a sudden spike in new failures. New reports continue after that merge. “Known” does not mean resolved. Keep investigating new symptoms and update the counts from new data rather than treating this export as live.

Replacement triage rule

  1. Read the report body. Resolve the tenant’s actual locale and form from the tenant directory; do not infer them from browser locale.
  2. Compare the affected path and cause. Merge only when the cause is established as the same; subjects may differ.
  3. Keep different causes apart, even when subjects match. Unknown tenant or contradictory symptom means investigate, not automatic merging.
  4. On the date parsing path, use the parser ticket. On booking-v3 form input for a day-first tenant, use the separate hint ticket. Preserve exceptions for investigation.
  5. Deduplicate export rows by report ID. Retain the report’s received time separately from merge time.
  6. Re-read new arrivals while work is in progress. Reconcile this decision, the incident page and the triage entry point when the desk changes; an old accepted decision is superseded by a new record, never rewritten.
